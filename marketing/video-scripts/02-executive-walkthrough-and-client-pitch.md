### Segment 3: The Proof of Concept — Live Deep Dive (1:30 - 3:00)
*(Full-screen recording of the SDR & FPGA Pacer platform at `https://embedded-sdr-fpga-roadmap.vercel.app`)*

> **Macharia Ndichu (Voiceover over screen demo):**  
> "Welcome to the **SDR & FPGA Pacer**, engineered by Nexus.  
> First, notice how the curriculum is organized: a clean, 5-phase progressive journey where learners move from mathematical theory to real-world hardware deployment.  
> But here is where the magic happens. Look at our interactive modules.  
> 
> *[Visual: Cursor opens Nyquist-Shannon Sampling simulator]*  
> Instead of staring at the equation $f_s \ge 2 f_{max}$, the learner grabs the sampling slider. As they pull the sampling rate below the Nyquist threshold, they instantly watch the reconstructed sine wave distort into an alias right before their eyes. When they toggle the Anti-Aliasing Filter, they immediately understand why analog front-ends exist.  
> 
> *[Visual: Cursor navigates to IQ Modulation Simulator]*  
> Next, take In-Phase and Quadrature (I/Q) modulation. Most engineers struggle with constellation points for months. Here, the student switches between BPSK, QPSK, and 16-QAM in one click. They inject AWGN noise, introduce phase skew, and see the constellation cloud disperse in real time alongside time-domain RF carriers.  
> 
> *[Visual: Cursor navigates to AXI-Stream Protocol Visualizer]*  
> Even low-level digital design protocols like AXI4-Stream become completely transparent. The learner steps the clock cycle-by-cycle, watching TVALID, TREADY, backpressure, and pipeline stalls resolve interactively.  
> 
> *[Visual: Cursor enters the Field Codex & Thesaurus]*  
> And whenever they encounter unfamiliar terms, our built-in **Field Codex & Thesaurus** provides instant searchable definitions, LaTeX mathematical proofs, and cross-referenced linkages."
