## 🛠️ Recording & Technical Checklist

1. **Resolution & Framing:**
   - 1080 × 1920 (9:16 Vertical).
   - Keep key text and faces inside the **"Safe Zone"** (the middle 80% of the screen so TikTok/Reels UI buttons like Like, Comment, and Share do not obscure text).
2. **Audio Quality:**
   - Use a lapel / lavalier microphone (e.g., DJI Mic, Rode Wireless, or clear phone audio).
   - Clear voiceover audio is responsible for 70% of viewer retention on short-form platforms.
3. **Screen Recording:**
   - Record the browser demo on a high-DPI display with 125% browser zoom so UI text and sliders are razor sharp on mobile screens.
   - Smooth mouse movement: use smooth gestures when dragging the Nyquist slider and clicking between BPSK and 16-QAM in the IQSimulator.
4. **Captions & Visual Hooks:**
   - Always include dynamic animated captions (using CapCut, Premiere Pro Auto-Captions, or Submix).
   - Highlight power words in **Electric Cyan (`#38bdf8`)** or **Vibrant Yellow (`#facc15`)** to capture silent scrollers.
