   "If we can make FPGA Digital Signal Processing intuitive in 15 minutes, we can do the exact same thing for your curriculum—whether in Medicine, Quantitative Finance, Aviation, Robotics, Computer Science, or Law."
3. **The Brand:**  
   Prominently positions **Nexus** as the premier custom learning engine development agency and **Tech by Macharia Ndichu** as the technical visionary and creator behind it.
